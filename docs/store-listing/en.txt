Blab Translation — AI translation for web pages, PDFs, manga and subtitles.

Read web pages, PDFs, ebooks, manga and comics with the original and the translation side by side, watch YouTube with dual subtitles, and translate the text in any image (OCR). Pick one of three engines: Chrome's free on-device translator, the AI you trust — cloud or local models, with your own key — or Blab Translation, included with a subscription, no key needed.

★ BILINGUAL WEB PAGES
• Translation under every paragraph. Formatting, links and images untouched.
• Translation only? One key: Alt+T.
• Main content first — menus and sidebars stay out of the way. Alt+W for the whole page.
• Only want one part? Point at an area and choose "Only translate here".
• Reaches inside iframes and Shadow DOM.
• Math, LaTeX and code stay intact. Papers stay readable.
• 6 translation styles. 76 languages. Right-to-left done right.

★ AUTO-TRANSLATE THAT KNOWS WHEN TO STOP
• Sentences already in your language are skipped — even when packed with foreign terms.
• Private messages are never auto-translated.
• Site rules: point at any area — "Don't translate here" or "Keep original". Pin an engine, add CSS. Rules sync with your browser account.

★ PDF TRANSLATION THAT KEEPS THE LAYOUT
• Bilingual or translation-only PDFs, with figures, formulas and layout exactly where they were.
• Free pages every month with sign-in.

★ EBOOKS & DOCUMENTS
• Word, EPUB, TXT and Markdown → bilingual or translated files.
• MOBI and AZW3 read bilingually in the web reader.

★ DUAL SUBTITLES
• YouTube subtitles translated live: original and translation, two lines.
• Works with any HTML5 player that has a standard subtitle track.
• One-click toggle right in the player's control bar.

★ SELECT, HOVER, LOOK UP
• Select text to translate — retranslate, switch engine, copy, read aloud.
• Built-in dictionary for words and phrases: pronunciation, senses, examples, word forms.
• The languages you picked last sit at the top of the language menu.
• Hold a modifier key and hover to translate a single paragraph.
• Typing in a different language from the page? Translate it with one click.

★ MANGA & COMICS
• Manga and comic pages redrawn in your language — and colorized if you like.

★ TEXT IN ANY IMAGE (OCR)
• Right-click any image — a screenshot, a photo, a chart — to read the text in it and translate it.
• The whole image, or just an area you draw. Large images also show a "Recognize Text" button when you hover.
• Read offline on your device: English, plus Chinese, Japanese or Korean when that is your interface language — or by a vision model with your own key for other languages, photos and stylized lettering.
• With a subscription, Blab Translation reads them too — no key needed.

★ PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Web pages translate on your device by default. Your text never leaves your computer.
• With your own AI model, text goes straight to your endpoint with your own key — never through us.
• Blab Translation sends the text — or, for OCR, the image — to our servers to process it and keeps none of it.
• PDF, document and comic files are processed on our servers and deleted after 7 days.
• Offline dictionary packs are downloaded from our server and kept on your device; that is what the unlimited storage permission is for. Looking a word up in a pack sends nothing.
• No analytics. No ad tracking.
